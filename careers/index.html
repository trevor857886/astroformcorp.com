CAREERS

Help rebuild how America makes metal.

Join the team building large metal structures for defense and space. You'll work on real hardware, side by side with the people who design, make and test it.

OPEN ROLES

Mechanical Engineer

ENGINEERING · FULL TIME

Design and analyze metal structures for defense and space customers, from early concepts through testing. You'll work across CAD, analysis and hands-on prototyping, and turn customer requirements into hardware that performs.

Manufacturing Engineer

MANUFACTURING · FULL TIME

Own how our structures get made, from cutting and welding to forming, inspection and quality. You'll improve our processes and equipment, solve problems on the shop floor, and help scale production as our programs grow.

Project Manager

PROGRAMS · FULL TIME

Lead customer programs from kickoff to delivery. You'll manage schedules, budgets and requirements, keep engineering, production and customers aligned, and make sure hardware ships on time.
